What This Means

A Dusk is not an engineered attack, a political action, or a declaration of hostility. It is a naturally occurring cosmological disaster whose onset cannot be scheduled, negotiated, or prevented by ordinary means.

Most known Dusks are limited in duration. The present forecast is different. Current models repeatedly converge on an extended interval long enough to affect continuity, observation, communication, and the stability of ordinary records.

Human observers should treat this notice as an early warning, not as a countdown. The exact beginning remains uncertain.
